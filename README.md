# healpick

Restored health pickups in free-roam, and adrenaline pickups

I remember that Kaizo M made a video about the health pickups, and so I tried 
to restore them. Because the map might have changed since they were last 
placed, quite a few of them would be in unreachable places or not in the 
right place, so I moved them and some others. If I remember it right, some of 
them do appear in missions, so I didn't 'restore' those ones

Currently there's health pickups at every hospital and hidden in some 
other places. See the Kaizo M video for the health pickups, but I did move 
some of them close-by

As for adrenaline, I placed them based on an old guide, wherever I thought it 
makes sense, or at least close to what's mentioned

I didn't check all the pickups yet, there's 50 health pickups and 10 adrenaline 
pickups in total, it's possible some of them may be too close to the ground. 
Pickup coords are saved with no decimal precision, and I'm yet not sure if 
the number is rounded or not. Anyway, I removed the precision from all coords but 
didn't check what it did

Also they shouldn't get stuck in savegames, which means there shouldn't be 
multiple pickups in the same location (unless you're using another mod that 
does the same thing)

I know there may or may not be other mods that would do the same thing I did, 
but this one is mine and I know what it does :3
